## AMAZON KDP LISTING FORMULA

### Book Description Structure (ABCDE Formula)
**A — Attention** (hook line — one sentence that grabs)
**B — Build** (describe the problem the reader is facing)
**C — Credibility** (why this book/author is the solution)
**D — Details** (what's inside — bullet points of key chapters/benefits)
**E — End** (call to action: "Get your copy today")

### Category Strategy
- Use Publisher Rocket to find categories where BSR #1 = 20,000–100,000
- Select 2 categories in KDP; request additional categories via email to KDP support (you can get up to 10)
- Aim for categories with "Best Seller" badge at <500 sales/month

### Keyword Strategy (7 backend keywords)
- Mix: 1 broad keyword, 3 mid-tail, 3 long-tail specific
- Example for ADHD book:
  1. "ADHD adults" (broad)
  2. "ADHD productivity system" (mid-tail)
  3. "executive function strategies adults" (mid-tail)
  4. "how to focus with ADHD at work" (long-tail)
  5. "ADHD time management techniques" (long-tail)
  6. "adult ADHD self help book" (long-tail)
  7. "neurodivergent productivity system" (long-tail)
