# KDP Tools & Platforms Guide

## Research & Keyword Tools

| Tool | Price | Best For | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Publisher Rocket** | $199 one-time | Keywords, categories, competition, Amazon Ads | #1 ROI tool for KDP — buy this first |
| **Book Bolt** | $9.99–$19.99/mo | Low-content books (journals, planners, notebooks) | Essential for workbook strategy |
| **Kindlepreneur** | Free | KDP calculators, research articles, KENP data | Start here before paying for anything |
| **K-lytics** | $97/report | Full genre-level market research reports | Use before entering a new genre |

## Writing Software

| Tool | Price | Best For | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Atticus** | $147 one-time | Writing + formatting (cross-platform) | **Best all-in-one for new KDP authors** |
| **Scrivener** | $59 one-time | Organizing complex long-form projects | Best for organizing multi-chapter books |
| **Vellum** | $249 one-time | Beautiful formatting (Mac only) | Mac users only; gold standard for formatting |

## Editing & Proofreading

| Tool | Price | Best For | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ProWritingAid** | $399 lifetime | Deep editing, pacing, style analysis for authors | Best for long-form; buy lifetime |
| **Grammarly** | Free / $144/yr | Quick grammar/spelling everywhere | Use free tier for quick checks |
| **Hemingway Editor** | Free / $19.99 | Readability, passive voice, sentence complexity | Use for all non-fiction |

## AI Writing Assistance

| Tool | Price | Best For | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sudowrite** | $19–$99/mo | Fiction: scene expansion, plot brainstorming, writing in your voice | Best AI for fiction writers |
| **Jasper AI** | $49–$125/mo | Book descriptions, back cover blurbs, Amazon ad copy | Best AI for book marketing copy |
| **Claude / ChatGPT** | Free / $20/mo | Brainstorming, outlining, research summaries | Great for structure and planning |

## Cover Design

| Tool | Price | Best For | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Canva Pro** | $15/mo | Non-fiction covers, workbook covers | OK for non-fiction; avoid for fiction |
| **BookBrush** | $8–$16/mo | KDP-spec covers, 3D mockups, promo graphics | Better than Canva for book-specific needs |
| **Professional Designer** | $150–$600 | Fiction covers (genre-specific, high quality) | **Highest ROI investment for fiction** |

Recommended designer platforms: Reedsy, 99designs, The Book Cover Designer, Damonza

## Publishing & Distribution

| Platform | Cost | Market Share | Use For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Amazon KDP** | Free | ~60% of book market | Primary platform — always start here |
| **Draft2Digital** | 10% of sales | Apple, Kobo, B&N, libraries | Go wide after establishing on Amazon |
| **IngramSpark** | $49 setup | Physical bookstores, libraries | Long-term brand building |
| **PublishDrive** | Subscription | Global incl. Google Play | When ready to go truly global |

## Recommended Tool Stack for New KDP Author

### Phase 1 — Getting Started (Free / Low Cost)
- Kindlepreneur.com (free resources)
- Hemingway Editor (free)
- Grammarly (free tier)
- Amazon KDP (free)
- Canva (free for non-fiction covers)

### Phase 2 — First $500 Invested
- Publisher Rocket — $199 (keyword/category research)
- Atticus — $147 (writing + formatting)
- ProWritingAid — $99/year or save for $399 lifetime

### Phase 3 — Scaling Up
- Professional cover designer — $150–$600 per cover
- Sudowrite — $19/mo (if writing fiction)
- Jasper AI — $49/mo (for marketing copy)
- Draft2Digital (go wide after 3+ books on Amazon)
